# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Vacation & PTO
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly.
Vacation days must be requested in the HR portal at least 5 business days in
advance. Up to 5 unused vacation days roll over into the next calendar year.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. For absences longer than 3
consecutive days, a doctor's note is required. Sick days do not roll over.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require VP approval. All employees must be reachable on Slack during core
hours (10:00–15:00 in their local timezone).

## Expenses
Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Expenses over $500
require pre-approval from your manager. All receipts must be submitted within
30 days through the Expensify tool.

## Travel
Economy class is the standard for flights under 6 hours. Flights over 6 hours
may be booked as premium economy. Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night in
most cities; high-cost cities are capped at $300 per night.

## Security
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 90 days. Two-factor
authentication is mandatory on all company accounts. Laptops must be locked
when unattended and full-disk encryption is required.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 8 weeks. Leave can be taken within 12 months of the birth
or adoption.

## Equipment
New hires receive a laptop, monitor, and a $150 home-office stipend. Equipment
is refreshed every 3 years. Broken equipment should be reported to IT via the
helpdesk portal.

## Holidays
The company observes 11 public holidays per year, including New Year's Day,
Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. A floating holiday can be used
at any time during the year.

## Offboarding
Employees leaving the company must return all equipment within 5 business
days. Final paychecks are issued on the next regular pay cycle. Access to
company systems is revoked on the last working day.
